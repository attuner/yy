Our Grain Philosophy

Dedicated to reviving India's ancient super-grains: Sorghum (Jowar), Finger Millet (Ragi), Pearl Millet (Bajra), and Foxtail Millet. We strictly avoid wheat flour, Maida, adulterants, and artificial preservatives.

Every roti is hand-patted live, roasted on cast iron tawas, and served fresh at our pickup counter.

100% Gluten-Free Millets: Pure stone-ground millet flour prepared under clean hygienic protocols.
Iron Tawa Roasting: Baked on cast iron for authentic rustic aroma and mineral retention.
Cold-Pressed Oils & Cultured Curd: No refined palm oils or artificial fats. Fresh probiotic earthen buttermilk.
Direct Pickup Model: Zero third-party aggregator markups, passing savings to you through prepaid wallet discounts.
